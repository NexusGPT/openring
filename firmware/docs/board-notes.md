## 7. The pin check and the stages

`tools/check_ring_pins.py <zephyr.dts> <netlist.csv> [--source-netlist <path>]` parses the built devicetree with the
kit's dtlib, decodes pin-control values with the bit layout read from the kit's `nrf-pinctrl.h`, and checks every U1
net: 28 port-pin signals by their GPIO property or pin-control function (every state, default and sleep), 8
fixed-function signals by their enabled node (lfxo, hfxo, usbhs, radio), 10 nets with no node by nature (supply,
ground, decoupling, debug, reset), 10 rows with no net. Fix round 1 (S7-03): what it sees, exactly: the pin of
every GPIO property and pin-control function of an enabled node, and the GPIO flags ACTIVE_LOW, PULL_UP, PULL_DOWN of
the 11 GPIO signals a source names (the other 7 print "no source"); it fails on a pin of an enabled node its table does
not account for, on a gpio hog, on a property ending in `gpios` it cannot read (UNEXPECTED), on an enabled ADC,
comparator or NFC node, whose pins it cannot see (UNSEEN), and on a U1 net it has no rule for (UNLISTED). It does not
see a pin a driver selects in code without the devicetree, and for the 8 fixed-function signals it proves only that
