33:Screen power sequence (sch-notes sections 2 and 7 item 2, and section 9 item 1). VCI (VDD_3V0) and IOVCC (VDD_1V8) are on whenever the bucks run. The SGM38046 is in shutdown only when AVDDEN (OLED_PWR_EN) and SWIRE are both low; SWIRE is driven by the SH8501A, not by the nRF.
34:- On (Tailor sheet page 9): (1) LCD_RST low; VCI and IOVCC are already on (no order between them); (2) OLED_PWR_EN high (SG Micro allows AVDDEN before SWIRE); (3) wait 10 ms or more; (4) LCD_RST high, wait 10 ms or more; (5) send the vendor start-up code and DCS "sleep out", wait 120 ms or more; (6) DCS "display on"; the SH8501A starts SWIRE and ELVDD, ELVSS 66.7 ms or more after "display on": draw nothing that matters before that.
35:- Off: (1) DCS "display off"; (2) DCS "sleep in", wait 120 ms (the SH8501A stops SWIRE); (3) OLED_PWR_EN low; (4) LCD_RST low. The SGM38046 is then in shutdown (both inputs low).
36:- While the screen is off, OLED_PWR_EN is low in every state; LCD_SCK, LCD_MOSI, LCD_CS and LCD_DCX stay at their idle levels (IOVCC is on, so no line back-powers the screen).
