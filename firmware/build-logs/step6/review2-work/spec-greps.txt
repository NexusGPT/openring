== main loop
1065:- Watchdog: `wdt31`, 8 s timeout, fed every 1 s by a work item on the system work queue (the product has no main loop). While a recording runs it is fed only when the audio and storage threads have both reported progress in the last 2 s; with no recording it is fed at every turn. A stalled system work queue, or a recording thread without progress, ends in a reset after 8 s.
