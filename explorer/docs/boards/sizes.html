Three ring sizes, drawn to the same scale

Each drawing shows the ring from above on a finger 18 mm wide, then from the fingertip. The bright rectangle is the lit part of the screen. One square of the grey grid is 5 mm.

Why not 22 x 15 mm: no colour screen on sale is that small. The reference ring's own screen is 0.9 inch, so its lit area alone is about 19 x 13 mm. The two slim options below use the two smallest colour screens on sale.

Battery and hour figures are estimates until a prototype is measured. Hours are given at the power draw of a Plaud recorder (13 mA), then at my lower estimate for this design.
