Battery in the band: how thin the block gets

Same slab every time: 28.6 mm long, 14.05 mm wide, black glass from edge to edge. Only the height changes. Top drawing: seen from the side on a finger 18 mm thick. Bottom drawing: seen from the fingertip. One grey square is 5 mm.

You said: “all battery not in housing but in ring”. With the battery in the band, the block holds only the screen, its glass, the circuit board and the floor: about 3.2 mm. The vibration motor is 2.1 mm thick and raises the block to 4.1 mm. The band grows to hold a curved battery.

Battery and hour figures are estimates until a prototype is measured. Hours are given at my estimated current for this design (4 mA), then at the current of a Plaud recorder (13 mA). A thin curved cell stores less per volume than a thick flat one; the ranges include that. The curved battery is a custom order, like the flat one before it.
