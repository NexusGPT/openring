Idea: choosing a made part puts that part alone on the stage, big, turnable, with its measurements drawn on it and its drawing one click away in the same space; the ring shrinks to a corner to show where it sits, and the panel keeps the plain words and the four files.

Named axis: what the stage shows once a part is chosen. Here: the part itself, on its own, with its measurements; the ring becomes a small inset.

Worse at: seeing the part in its place. While a part is open the ring is only a small corner picture, so understanding how parts fit together, or hopping from one neighbouring part to the next, takes a step back to the whole ring first.

Change to the app layout (the one allowed): L1 says the 3D ring is always on the left. Here the ring stays on screen but shrinks to an inset while a part is open. Cost: the owner loses the large in-place view while reading a part, and the part-to-ring transition is one more animation to build well.
