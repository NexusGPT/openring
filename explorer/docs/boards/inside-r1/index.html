Labelled boxes only: where things sit, not how they look. Picked by default while you were away: C. Tell me A or B and I rebuild.

A · The ring stays whole

The whole ring stays on screen with the chosen part lit; the card on the right gains a "For a maker" block. Drawn by a designer who saw the current screen.

B · The part on the bench

The chosen part alone takes the 3D area, with its measurements on it; the ring shrinks to a corner. Drawn by a designer who saw the current screen.

C · The ring above, the drawing below (picked by default)

The ring stays on top; for a made-to-drawing part a strip with its measured drawing slides up under it; the panel holds the plain words, then the maker's files. Drawn by a designer who never saw the current screen.
