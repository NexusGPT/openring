Idea: selecting a made-to-drawing part slides its measured drawing up under the 3D ring, so the owner sees where the part sits and the maker sees its sizes and takes its files, in one look on one screen; axis: what the stage shows when a part is selected (the part in the ring and its drawing together, not the ring alone).

Worse at: while a made part is selected the 3D ring loses about two fifths of its height; the stage changes shape between made and bought parts; every made part needs flat drawing views exported as pictures.

The one change to the shared layout: the left stage holds a drawing strip under the ring when a made part is selected. Cost: this screen is the only one whose 3D area splits in two.
