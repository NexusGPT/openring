Ring Explorer: three layout options

Boxes only: no colours, no real content yet. Each option shows two screens, Home and Inside. They differ in one thing: where the 3D ring lives and how you move between the 8 areas. You pick the layout first; the look comes after.

L1 · Stage

The 3D ring stays on screen at all times, large, on the left. The right side changes with the area you choose. Clicking a part in the 3D view and reading about it happen side by side.

Worse at: wide documents. The wiring diagram and the parts table get the narrower right side unless you expand them.

L2 · Pages

A top bar with the 9 screens. Each screen is its own full page with the layout that suits it: a big 3D view on Home, Look, Inside and Case; full-width documents on Electronics, Board and Parts.

Worse at: continuity. The ring leaves the screen when you open a document page, so the link between a part and its place in the ring takes one more click.

L3 · Story

One long page you scroll, like a product page. The ring stays pinned in the middle and changes as you scroll: first the outside, then it opens up, then the electronics, the behaviour, the case. An index on the left jumps to any chapter. Details open as side sheets.

Worse at: going straight to one thing. Finding a single part or a file means scrolling or using the index and search, and the parts table lives in a side sheet.
