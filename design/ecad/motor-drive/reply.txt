Recommendation (one): take the motor off the power chip. M1 gets its own 3.0 V regulator U11, TI TLV75530PDQNR (X2SON-4, 1.0 x 1.0 x 0.4 mm max), input on VBAT, output VMOT, EN from MOTOR through R61, with R62 as the pull-down. C83 and C84 are 10 uF 0402 Murata GRM155R60J106ME15. Q1 and D1 are removed. IBATLIM sits in the CHARGER path between VBAT and VSYS (spec p27, p6 block diagram). A load on the battery side of VBAT is outside it, like U4 PVIN already is (make_netlist.py line 275).

Why nothing else works: a stalled W0525 at its 2.5 V start voltage needs about 364.6 mW. Through the chip, 80 mA (a 20 % margin) gives at most 313.2 mW at 4.35 V and 237.6 mW at 3.30 V. A 10 ms start needs 3261 uF of capacitor, which is 296.4 parts of 22 uF 0402. A resistor that lets the motor run still lets 145 mA through at stall. Chopping a motor that has its own drive IC is outside Jinlong's spec (p5, p14). The nPM1304 outputs all sit behind the same limit. No 5 mm part on file has a lower start current. With no change, a start draws 134.1 to 188.2 mA from the cell.

Current through the chip with the recommendation: screen 55 mW + radio 15 mW + recording 4.0 mA + a storage write = 55.65 mA at the 3.1 V cut-off. That is a 44.35 % margin under 100 mA, whatever the motor does. Vibration claim: the maker's 0.25 G at 3.0 V. It is the same as today, unmeasured in the ring.
