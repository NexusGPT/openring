With the stand-in 3.45 V for the open-circuit voltage at 20 percent (see `pass-lines.md`, no file gives it) that is 1.9565 ohm. At that resistance the bench gives 2.99503 V (limit 3.0 V) and a peak cell current of 232.5 mA, not 230 mA. The largest cell resistance that passes is 1.9277 ohm at 3.45 V (R1.1 needs 1.9565). At 3.30 V (the firmware cut-off B2) it is 1.2213 ohm; at 3.60 V it is 2.7062 ohm. The margin is small and has a known cause: with C84 (10 uF) the dip is the moment U11 finishes charging C84 while the motor draws its start current. The settled cell voltage before the end of the start is 3.0232 V (passes by 23 mV, 3.0232 V minus 3.0 V). `results/sensitivity.txt`: with C84 at 4.7 uF, 3.3 uF or 2.0 uF the dip disappears (3.0232 V). The solver check in the same file gives 2.99458 to 2.99678 V over five solver settings, so the number is stable to 2.2 mV. `motor-drive.md` section 2.3 estimates that a 10 uF X5R 0402 part keeps a third to a half of its value at half its rating, so the real dip may be smaller; I did not model that derating. Verdict: the pass line is missed by 5 mV (3.0 V minus 2.99503 V) with the files as they are; treat it as marginal, not as a failure of the circuit.
