## The screen-face parts in the thin block (added after the lead's note)

The routed board carries on its screen face J1 and eight 0201 parts, C1, C2, C5, C7, C14, FB1, R71, R72, plus 12 flat test pads (`design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/read_face_b.log:1` to `design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/read_face_b.log:10`). The 0201 height used by the inside model is 0.33, marked ASSUMED there (`design/mech/thin/model-v10-notes.md:76`). The thin stack gives 0.10 of foam between the board top and the panel (`design/mech/thin/out/v10/before/placement.csv:23`), so as the board stands neither J1 nor the 0201 parts fit (`design/mech/thin/model-v10-notes.md:330`).

(1) What it takes to leave them where they are (board top 1.75 to panel front 2.65 is 0.9: `design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/calc.log:58`):
- The eight 0201 parts stay if the panel's back is at least land 0.02 + part 0.33 + clearance 0.05 over the board top, at 2.15: gap 0.4, panel at most 0.5 (`design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/calc.log:59`; the 0.05 clearance is the one the inside model keeps over J1: `design/mech/thin/model-v10-notes.md:96`). With a clearance of 0.10 instead: panel at most 0.45 (`design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/calc.log:60`). The foam frame round the board edge then is 0.4 thick instead of 0.10.
- J1 cannot stay on the screen face at any panel thickness: it needs 0.97 above the board at its nominal 0.9 and 1.07 at its maximum, against the 0.9 room: panel at most -0.07 or -0.17 (`design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/calc.log:61`, `design/mfg/specs/screen-panel-work/logs/calc.log:62`). So J1 moves in every option.
- Is a panel of 0.5 or less plausible from the research on disk? Every module thickness read from a datasheet is 0.7 or more: 0.8 (`design/parts/thin-bezel-parts.md:37`), 0.75 (`design/parts/thin-bezel-parts.md:38`), 0.71 typical and 0.80 maximum for a flexible folded-drive part (`design/parts/thin-bezel-parts.md:39`), 0.78 (`design/parts/thin-bezel-parts.md:40`), 0.7 (`design/parts/thin-bezel-parts.md:41`), and a maker overview's "thinnest thickness of 0.7 mm" (`design/parts/full-face-screen.md:107`). The only figures under 0.7 are a search snippet of 0.5 that could not be read on its page (`design/parts/full-face-screen.md:87`, `design/parts/full-face-screen.md:128`) and a listing of 0.3 whose outline equals its lit area, marked suspicious (`design/parts/full-face-screen.md:96`). So 0.5 is not shown possible by the research: it must be asked of the maker.
