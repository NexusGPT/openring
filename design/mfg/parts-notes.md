The files come from the inside model v7 (`design/mech/thin/thin.py`, section "Model v7" of `design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md`) built once per ring size with the same exterior and bore as that size's fit check: `design/look/s1/out/ring-merged32h-usN.step` at every size (bores 17.3196, 18.1324, 18.9452, 19.758, 20.5708, 21.3836, 22.1964). Export logs: `design/mech/thin/out/v7-export/export_us7.log` to `export_us13.log`; each written file's solid count and volume are checked against the model's own at export time (lines "STEP ...").

Written by `parts/export_parts.py` (it runs `design/mech/thin/out/v7-export/export_size.py` once per size), listed in `parts/index.csv`, verified by `parts/check_parts.py` (run both from `design/mfg/parts/` with `../../.venv/bin/python`). Units mm. Coordinates are the model's (ring axis along X, block on top, +Y the side away from the joystick), not re-centred. STEP is the exact geometry. STL is meshed at linear tolerance 0.01 mm and angular 0.1 rad, zero-area slivers removed; every STL is closed and manifold (index.csv "watertight").

The old files (exterior merged32c, body without cavities, the record cap, the cavity reference) are kept in `parts/old_merged32c/`, with the old index, exporter and logs. Do not make anything from them.

What no file here carries: tolerances, surface finish, coatings, material, process, threads, the copper of the boards and flex tails. Those are in `design/mfg/specs/<part>.md`. Where a spec line and these files disagree, the spec in `design/mfg/specs/` is the order and the difference is a defect to report.

## Per ring size (`<part>/<part>-usN.step`, N = 7 to 13)
- **body** (STEP and STL): the one-piece metal body WITH every inside pocket cut: panel pocket, cable fold pocket, microphone floor pockets (holes and seats come from the exterior), the two floor slots 2.1 x 0.6 at X -3.55 to -1.45 (inside model v8), the band channel (7.0 wide since v8), the hand-end corner on the +Y side cut away (it is the resin part RC1, below), the joystick pocket 7.0 x 6.8 with its roof 0.05 over the flange, the motor pocket (floor 0.05 under the motor), on US 7, 8 and 9 the motor window, the glass seat lowered 0.05. One solid, valid, per size. Its volume equals `outer_body` of that size's model within 0.0004 mm3 and its bounding box equals the exterior's within 0.0000 mm (export logs). Each body sheet carries a table of the pockets from the model (`body/body-usN.dims.csv`). A 3D printer can make it as a fit and look sample with the real pockets (a resin print shows whether the board, cell and motor go in); it is not the metal part: no tolerance or wall of a printed part is the machined one.
- **band-liner** (STEP and STL): the 0.3 mm resin skin with the fill under the joystick stiffener; the ribbons and the four pads are cut out of it. A print shows the cast shape; the real liner is cast in place.
- **cell** (STEP): the pouch body only (rounded, sealed edge and tabs not in it); its length on the centre line is in the index status. The volume of this solid is smaller than the 1.6 x 6.0 x length figure of the notes because of the 0.3 rounding and the 2.0 sealed edge left out.
- **flex-tail-thumb**, **flex-tail-away** (STEP): the bent ribbons as drawn in `design/ecad/band`, 1.8 mm wide at the neck, 0.11 thick outside the liner and 0.15 in it (inside model v8; `specs/band-flex-tails.md` section Files: 6 solids for FLEX1, 5 for FLEX2), and the polyimide foot stiffener of each tail as its own file (`flex-tail-thumb-footstiffener-usN.step`, `flex-tail-away-footstiffener-usN.step`, 0.10 thick). No copper, no layer stack: the flex maker needs its own artwork; these give the shape and length.
- **joystick-stiffener** (STEP): the flat 6.4 x 6.4 x 0.10 polyimide stiffener under the FLEX1 head (0.075 before model v8).
- **joystick-dam** (STEP): the dam as cured round the switch head.
- **glass-seal** (STEP): the 0.05 seal ring; the same at every size (2.9334 mm3 each), written per size for completeness.
- **motor-window-seal** (STEP, US 7, 8, 9 only): the sealant plug in the motor window.
- **assembly/ring-assembly-usN.step**: the whole ring of that size, every part placed (48 parts on US 7, 8, 9; 47 on US 10 to 13; 417 solids on US 7, 8, 9 and 416 on US 10 to 13, the same count as the model; `index.csv`), solids named `<part>__<class>` as in the model. The joystick cap and frame sit at -84.0 (US 7), -81.0 (US 8) and -78.0 deg (US 9 to 13).

## The same at every size (`<part>/<part>.step`, from the US 9 build)
- **cover-glass** (STEP): the exterior's formed glass. Its print has no opening in v7 (the print window is removed in v7); the print is not geometry.
- **joystick-cap** (STEP and STL): CAP1, the round cap: shank dia 4.40 with its rounded top edge, spherical dish and three ridges, and its 6.90 x 6.40 x 0.40 flange; one moulded part, written as two touching solids (shank and flange, 78.7324 mm3 together). The two solids are ONE part with no joint, no parting line and no weld: they touch on one disc, the shank's lower end face (diameter 4.40) lying on the flange's top face (the model builds the shank from the flange top upward and the flange 0.40 downward from the same plane, `design/mech/thin/thin.py`, the joystick cap block). Do not read the file as two parts to join; make CAP1 as one piece (`specs/joystick-nub.md`). The file is to be rewritten as one solid (list at the end of this page). Placed at the US 9 position (-78 deg); the same part at every size (the shank from the flange top to the rim plane is 4.110 to 4.118 mm in the seven size builds, within the model's 0.01 surface probe step; export logs, line "joystick US"). Spec: `specs/joystick-nub.md`.
- **joystick-frame** (STEP and STL): FRM1, the round frame ring, outer dia 5.40, bore 4.80, 1.30 long; placed at the US 9 position. Spec: `specs/joystick-nub.md`.
- **main-board** (STEP): the bare board core, 27.2 x 12.6, no notch (since model v9; the antenna notch of earlier versions is gone) and no hole. No mask, pads, copper or parts: a board house needs Gerber and drill files.
- **radio-corner** (STEP and STL): RC1, the resin radio corner of the body (X -14.80 to -8.60, Y 2.05 to the outer skin, 6.05 x 5.325 x 2.75, 26.4218 mm3, with the antenna floor pocket 0.20 deep), the same at every size; the body files have this corner cut away. Resin: Master Bond EP30Med, as the liner, cast on its own and bonded to the body before the board goes in (`specs/body.md` section Radio corner RC1; `assembly/assembly.md` step 10).
- **mic-gasket**, **mic-membrane** (STEP): both gaskets (at the nominal gap) and both membrane discs, 2 solids each.
