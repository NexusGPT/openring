# Dam material for DAM1: maker sheets, five candidates

Scope: the dam is a bead dispensed from a 0.25 mm needle round the switch stack in the joystick pocket, before the EP30Med liner cast (model-v10-notes.md, "Point 6 worked out"). The requirements taken from that section and from band-liner.md (Material, Process) and deferred-facts.md (Items 112 and 115): a non-slump thixotropic paste that bridges 0.30 mm and stands 0.56 mm high over up to 0.79 mm open depth; cure in place at room temperature within minutes, by light from the bore side, with a second cure for the shadow under the head; never above 45 C; low shrinkage; bond to polyimide and the coated body; the liner resin must bond to it and not dissolve it.

Method: five maker sheets saved and converted to text (design/datasheets/dam_*.pdf and .txt). Every value below carries `p.N l.L [ID]`: page N and text line L of the saved text copy, and the quote ID in design/mfg/assembly/dam-work/quotes.tsv. The check `verify-quotes.sh` finds each quote on its cited line: quotes checked 156, not found 0 (quotes-verify.log). "not stated" means the sheet, searched in full, does not give it (not-stated-search.log). No recommendation is made.

## Per candidate

**Dymax 9014** (dam_dymax_9014.pdf, rev. 02/10/2023). It meets: a light cure that works in seconds (p.1 l.13), a stated moisture cure for shadow areas with times at 25 C and at 40 C (p.2 l.80-81; 40 C is under the 45 C limit), a linear shrinkage of 1.8 (p.1 l.49), and Kapton under recommended surfaces (p.1 l.6). The sheet does not say polyimide; that Kapton is a polyimide film is general knowledge, not from the sheet. It does not meet, or the sheet does not state: any thixotropic index or slump wording (only 18,000 cP nominal, p.1 l.26); the bridge and stand-height values; a metal or titanium substrate; an overcoat or epoxy-bond statement; a service range (Tg 51, p.1 l.50); a package size. The sheet says "industrial use only" (p.4 l.150).

**Dymax 9422-SC** (dam_dymax_9422-sc.pdf, data collected before 2011). It meets: a thixotropic claim in words, "Highly Thixotropic for" (p.1 l.7) with "Minimal Movement After Dispense" (p.1 l.8), 38,000 cP nominal (p.1 l.29), a light cure in seconds (p.1 l.14), and a linear shrinkage of 1.6 (p.1 l.55). It does not meet, or does not state: a shadow cure (none; shadow only slows the cure, p.2 l.106); any thixotropic index; a bridge or stand value; a service range; polyimide or titanium (the adhesion table lists Flex and Leadframe, p.1 l.35-40, without readable recommendation marks); an overcoat statement; a package size. The sheet says "industrial use only" (p.3 l.139).

**Henkel LOCTITE SI 5240** (dam_henkel-loctite_si-5240.pdf, September 2020). It meets: a thixotropic product that "will thin with shear yet provides uniform cavity fill" (p.1 l.29-31), 15,000 to 35,000 mPa·s (p.1 l.50-51), a UV or visible cure at 70 mW/cm² for 60 s (p.2 l.107) with a depth of "≥10" (p.1 l.32), moisture cure for shadowed areas with the times 7 and 14 days (p.2 l.74, l.87), function almost instant (p.3 l.148), Shore A "≥ 40LMS" (p.2 l.114), and a 0.3 linear shrinkage (p.2 l.131). It does not meet, or does not state: a thixotropic index or bridge or stand values; a service range (aging at 150 C only, p.3 l.164); polyimide or titanium (steel and aluminium only in lap shear, p.2 l.116 and l.113); an overcoat statement; a package size. It names no medical grade: ISO 10993 testing "to assist in the selection" for the medical device industry (p.1 l.42-44).

**Hoenle (Vitralit UD 8060 F)** (dam_panacol_vitralit-ud8060f.pdf, Rev. 4, updated 06.08.2026; the sheet is issued by "Hoenle Adhesives GmbH", p.5 l.258). It is marked "Preliminary" (p.1 l.3) and says the values "ARE NOT MEANT TO BE USED AS PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS" (p.5 l.235). It meets: a thixotropic index of 2 at 6,000 mPas (p.2 l.63-66), a 405 nm LED cure with a 5 s row at 400 mW/cm² and 0.5 mm (p.1 l.28), a humidity shadow cure with complete cure in 8 days at 23 C and 49 % RH (p.1 l.39; p.3 l.156), strength at +8 days 21 MPa (p.3 l.153), a typical operating range of -40 to 130 C (p.2 l.80), and block shear on FR4, copper and AlMg3 (p.3 l.123; p.2 l.108, l.105). It does not meet, or does not state: shrinkage; hardness; polyimide; titanium; an overcoat or epoxy-bond statement; a package size; a medical statement (only RoHS, p.5 l.232). The sheet's 365 nm marks are not readable in the text copy (p.1 l.18).

**Henkel LOCTITE ABLESTIK UV300X** (dam_henkel-loctite_uv300x.pdf, December-2016). It meets: a thixotropic index of 3.8 (0.5/5 rpm, p.1 l.32), 70,000 cP at 2.5 rpm (p.1 l.31), an opaque slightly colored liquid gel (p.1 l.12), UV at 365 nm with irradiance ">50" (p.1 l.41-42), fixture 1 s and tack-free 5 s (p.1 l.43-44), a Shore D 78 hardness (p.1 l.10), and a smallest syringe of 3.0 ml (p.1 l.49). It does not meet: the shadow cure is heat only, "1hour @ 100°C or" and "2hours @ 85°C" (p.1 l.46-47), which is above the 45 C limit; the depth of cure has no number (p.1 l.48). It does not state: shrinkage; a service range (Tg 88, p.1 l.11, is not a service limit); polyimide; metals beyond alumina to glass (p.1 l.24); an overcoat or epoxy-bond statement; a medical grade.
