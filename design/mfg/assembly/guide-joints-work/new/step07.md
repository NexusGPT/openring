### Step 7. Solder the motor's lead wires to FLEX1

- **Join:** M1 (`vibration_motor`, Jinlong W0525AB001D) to FLEX1, the red lead to the + land and the blue lead to the - land, by hand soldering (`design/architecture.md:597`, `design/architecture.md:605`). FLEX1 has no mark on it. The + land is the land nearer the slot: land 1 of the motor lands, net VMOT, joined to land 7 (MOT+) of J_BAND_A. The - land is the other one: land 2, net GND, joined to land 6 (MOT-) of J_BAND_A (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:123`). The red lead is the + lead (`design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:24`).
- **How:** solder: Kester 275 SAC305 wire with a no-clean core, 0.25 mm, part 2470687623 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:63`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:65`). Flux: the wire's core only; the residue stays (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:70`). Iron: 50 W, temperature controlled, chisel tip 0.8 mm wide or narrower (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:150`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:176`). Heat: 320 C, 2 s per joint (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:125`). 1. Tin the stripped lead end with the same wire. 2. Lay it along the 0.80 mm side of its land. 3. Touch the land and the lead together with the tip. 4. Feed the wire to the joint, never to the tip (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:146`). 5. Lift the iron within 2 s.
- **Locate:** the motor pads of FLEX1 beside the motor's place, 0.7 mm off the band's centre line (0.8 mm at US 7 and US 12) at the motor angle of the ring size: -44.0 degrees at US 9, -47.0 at US 7, -42.5 at US 13 (motor angles US 7 to 13: -47.0, -45.5, -44.0, -45.0, -43.5, -42.5, -42.5; `design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md` model v6.1 and v6.2; `design/architecture.md:783` to `design/architecture.md:789`; table above).
- **Before:** step 6 passed. Cut each lead to the length of its ring size, measured from the motor body edge to the lead end: US 7 1.12 mm, US 8 1.07, US 9 1.08, US 10 1.14, US 11 1.39, US 12 1.44, US 13 1.39 (`design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:24`, `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:25`, `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:125`). Strip 0.6 mm (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:125`).
- **Check:** both joints pass the accept rules of step 2. Apply 3.0 V through a 200 mA current limit to the ribbon's board end, + on land 7 (VMOT) and - on land 6 (GND) of the FLEX1 foot, numbered on the flex drawing as in step 6 (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:123`; change C13), for 0.5 s: the motor vibrates [PROPOSED: reason, the motor has its own drive chip, rated 3.0 V, start under 175 mA, `design/architecture.md:598`]. Use needle probe tips of 0.2 mm or finer (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:180`). Note the meter reading between land 7 and land 6: it is the motor's own reading, used again at step 14. In the ring the same lines carry VMOT from U11, red lead on VMOT (`design/architecture.md:705`, `design/bom.csv:44`).
- **After:** cut leads cannot be lengthened.
