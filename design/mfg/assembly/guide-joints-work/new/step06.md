### Step 6. Solder the five joystick switches on the ribbon FLEX1

- **Join:** SW1 (up), SW3 (down), SW4 (toward the hand), SW5 (toward the fingertip) and SW2 (centre) to the head of FLEX1 (`band_flex_thumb`), with solder paste and one reflow, the head flat on a carrier. In model v8 the head is flat, 6.4 x 6.4 x 0.11, and lies on a flat polyimide stiffener of 6.4 x 6.4 x 0.10 mm under it (0.15 and 0.075 mm in model v6), drawn as part of the ribbon (`design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:250`, `design/architecture.md:795`); the flex maker bonds the stiffener to the head before this step (`design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:250`; its material is polyimide, `design/mfg/specs/band-flex-tails.md`).
- **How:** 1. Lay the head flat on the carrier, switch face (F.Cu) up, stiffener down. 2. Lay the 0.08 mm stainless steel stencil of FLEX1 on the head (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:86`). It is cut from the FLEX1 paste layer, one 0.55 x 0.55 mm opening on each of the 12 switch lands (change C1, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:195`). 3. Print Kester NP505-HR paste (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:58`) with the squeegee of step 2. 4. Place each switch on its lands. 5. Reflow once in the oven on the profile of step 2 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:104`). The switches allow a peak of 260 C for 3 s and at most 2 reflows (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:130`); one pass is used. If no oven is at hand, the hot plate of step 8 follows the same profile (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:159`). 6. Use no liquid flux. The paste brings its own no-clean flux. Alps forbids flux on the switch face ("Upper face shall be free from flax prior to soldering.") and asks to keep flux out of the switch (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:67`). Do not clean the switches with a solvent (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:71`).
- **Locate:** the cross at 2.4 mm from the cap axis, SW2 at the centre, 0.30 mm to its neighbours (`design/architecture.md:588`, `design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:73`). The stencil by its openings over the 12 lands, seen under the 10 times magnifier (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:189`); a placing machine uses the three fiducials on the switch face of the FLEX1 panel (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:187`, change C4).
- **Before:** FLEX1 is made with its stiffener, in the length of its ring size (the joystick angle changes by size: table above). The outline exists: FLEX1 is drawn as a board for each size, `design/ecad/band/flex1-us7.kicad_pcb` to `design/ecad/band/flex1-us13.kicad_pcb` (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:100`). The switch land pattern is decided: Alps_SKTAAAE010, four lands for SW2 and two lands for each outer switch (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:102`, `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:103`). The FLEX1 paste layer exists in `design/ecad/band/fab/flex1-usN/` (change C1; today it is missing, so no stencil can be cut: review 3 note G7).
- **Check:** every switch joint passes the accept rules of step 2. Each switch closes its own line and no other, measured with an ohmmeter at the ribbon's board end (the FLEX1 foot) while it is pressed, and reads open (no connection) when it is released, so a switch that is closed all the time fails. The foot's lands carry the same numbers as J_BAND_A (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:65`); the flex drawing of each size, `design/ecad/band/fab/flex1-usN-drawing.pdf`, prints the number and net of each foot land (change C13, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:207`). Probe between land 8 (GND) and: land 1 (JOY_UP) for SW1, land 2 (JOY_DOWN) for SW3, land 3 (JOY_LEFT) for SW4, land 4 (JOY_RIGHT) for SW5, land 5 (BTN_REC) for SW2 (`design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:149` to `design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:156`). Use needle probe tips of 0.2 mm or finer, so one tip touches one 0.30 mm land and not the next (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:180`). Height 0.53 mm; every switch base flat on the head (the model gives 0.0000 mm, `design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:250`), checked with a feeler of 0.02 mm (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:181`).
- **After:** nothing irreversible; a switch can be reworked until step 15, when the harness goes into the channel.
