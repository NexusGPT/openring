### Step 9. Join the cell, its protection circuit and the thermistor to FLEX2

- **Join:** the three output tabs of the bought cell pack (BT1 with PRT1 (`cell_protection`) and RT1 inside it as one part, `design/bom.csv:48`; `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:34` to `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:36`) to the three lands of FLEX2, by hand soldering at low heat. RT1 is not a separate part and has no joint of its own. Tab P+ goes on land 1 (VBAT), tab NTC on land 3 (NTC), tab P- on land 2 (GND). The tabs lie in the order P+, NTC, P- from the slot toward the cell, which is lands 1, 3, 2 (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:111` to `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:115`). The joint is 0.2 mm from the cell (`design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:67`); the cell maker states the limit (S12).
- **How:** solder: Kester 275 SAC305 wire with a no-clean core, 0.25 mm, part 2470687623 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:65`; the lead's choice, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:82`). Flux: the wire's core only; the residue stays (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:70`). Iron: 50 W, temperature controlled, chisel or conical tip 0.45 mm wide or narrower (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:151`). Heat: 280 C at most, 2 s per joint at most (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:117`). 1. Measure the cell voltage before the first joint. 2. Lay each tab on its land. 3. Touch the land and the tab together with the tip. 4. Feed the wire to the joint, never to the tip (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:146`). 5. Lift the iron within 2 s. 6. Measure the cell voltage again after the last joint (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:117`).
- **Locate:** at US 9 the cell's tab end lies at +48.713 degrees and PRT1 starts at +39.5 degrees, 0.2 mm before the cell (`design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:264`, `design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:67`). Per size the cell's ends and PRT1's start change: PRT1 starts at 43.8 degrees on US 7 and 41.5 on US 8 (`design/architecture.md:794`; table above).
- **Before:** step 8 passed (the discs are on: the cell never sees the hot plate). The cell BT1 (`cell`) of the right size is delivered, 33.936 mm (US 7) to 46.121 mm (US 13) on its centre line (`design/architecture.md:783`, `design/architecture.md:789`; custom, S12, `design/architecture.md:508`). Its open-circuit voltage is measured. The protection limits are known from the cell maker (`design/bom.csv:48`).
- **Check:** the three joints pass the accept rules of step 2. The cell voltage appears at the ribbon's board end (the FLEX2 foot, lands numbered on the flex drawing as in step 8, change C13): red probe on land 1 (VBAT), black probe on land 2 (GND), with needle tips of 0.2 mm or finer (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:180`). The reading must be positive. If it is negative, first check on the drawing that the probes sit on lands 1 and 2; only if they do is a tab on the wrong land. The thermistor inside the pack reads about 10 k between land 3 (NTC) and land 2 (GND) at room temperature (`design/bom.csv:74`; `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:113`, `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:114`). The voltage after the joints is recorded: step 14 compares with it.
- **After:** from here the harness carries a live cell. The board end of FLEX2 must be covered with insulating tape until step 14 [PROPOSED: reason, a short there drains the cell through PRT1].
