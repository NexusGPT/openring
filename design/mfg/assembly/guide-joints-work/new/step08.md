### Step 8. Make the charging pads on FLEX2

- **Join:** CP1 (`pad_VBUS`), CP2 (`pad_D-`), CP3 (`pad_D+`), CP4 (`pad_GND`) to the four pad lands of FLEX2 (`band_flex_away`). The pad is a gold-plated disc, 1.6 mm across and 0.07 mm thick, soldered flat on its land with SAC305 paste at this step. The bare land is NOT the pad: its ENIG gold would wear through under the case's spring pins (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:141`; `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:30` to `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:33`). Four discs, one per land, in the order VBUS (CP1), D- (CP2), D+ (CP3), GND (CP4). Each disc has a via at the centre of its land (`design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:141`). In the finished ring the disc is flush with the liner's inner face, as the model has it (`design/mech/thin/placement.csv:43`); that is checked at step 21, once the liner exists. Parts list: row CP1 to CP4 of `design/bom.csv:64` (no maker order code yet: a stamped, hard-gold plated disc to these dimensions).
- **How:** paste through a stencil, then the hot plate (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:161`; confirmed by the lead, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:82`). 1. Lay the flat ribbon on a clean hot plate that is still cold, pad lands (B.Cu) up. 2. Lay the 0.08 mm stainless steel stencil of FLEX2 on the pad face (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:87`). It has four round openings of 0.58 mm on each disc land, which keep the paste off the centre via (change C2, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:196`). 3. Print Kester NP505-HR paste (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:58`) with the squeegee of step 2. Use no liquid flux. 4. Lay one disc on each land with fine non-magnetic tweezers, centred by eye under the 10 times magnifier (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:174`). 5. Tape a thermocouple on the ribbon beside a disc. 6. Heat the plate on the profile of step 2, read on that thermocouple: to 150 C at less than 2.5 C per second, 150 C to 200 C in 90 to 110 s, 60 to 75 s above 217 C, peak 240 C (235 to 245 C) (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:161`). 7. Slide the ribbon off the plate onto a metal block to cool. 8. Do not press a disc while the solder is liquid. This step comes before step 9, so the cell never sees this heat.
- **Locate:** four pads 2.4 mm apart along the arc, centred opposite the block, the row at X = -1.0 mm (toward the hand end of the block, ring -X; the case pins stand at X = -1.0, so a ring turned over in the case has its pads 2.0 mm from the pins and VBUS never meets GND; architecture 17.28; model v6.3), at 158.23, 172.74, 187.26 and 201.77 degrees at US 9, order VBUS, D-, D+, GND with rising angle (`design/mech/thin/thin-notes.md:75`); the pitch angle per size is 2.4 / (bore / 2) (table above). The stencil by its openings over the four lands, seen under the 10 times magnifier (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:189`); a placing machine uses the three fiducials on the pad face of the FLEX2 panel (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:187`, change C4).
- **Before:** FLEX2 is made. The outline exists: FLEX2 is drawn as a board for each size, `design/ecad/band/flex2-us7.kicad_pcb` to `design/ecad/band/flex2-us13.kicad_pcb`. The FLEX2 paste layer exists in `design/ecad/band/fab/flex2-usN/` (change C2; today it is missing). Four discs are at hand.
- **Check:** each disc joint passes the accept rules of step 2. Each disc lies flat on its land and is centred on it, with no tilt (a lifted edge fails). Height: the disc top stands 0.070 to 0.112 mm above the land copper, measured with a feeler gauge or a microscope with a focus scale in steps of 0.02 mm (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:163`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:181`). Continuity from each disc to its land at the ribbon's board end (the FLEX2 foot, lands numbered on the flex drawing `design/ecad/band/fab/flex2-usN-drawing.pdf`, change C13): VBUS to land 4, D- to land 5, D+ to land 6, GND to land 7 (`design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:30` to `design/wiring/product-wiring.csv:33`), with needle probe tips of 0.2 mm or finer (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:180`). No short between neighbouring discs or between their lands.
- **After:** nothing irreversible.
