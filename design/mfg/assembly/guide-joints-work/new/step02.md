### Step 2. Print paste, place the board parts on both faces, reflow twice (screen face first, then parts face)

- **Join:** the board parts of `design/bom.csv` to PCB1 in TWO solder passes, one per face, each with its own paste stencil and its own reflow. The board has parts on both faces (`design/ecad/ring/pcb/board-notes-r3.md:35`). PASS 1 is the SCREEN FACE and PASS 2 the PARTS FACE (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:84`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:85`; confirmed by the lead, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:82`). PASS 1, the SCREEN FACE (KiCad B.Cu, 21 parts in `design/ecad/ring/pcb/fab/ring-pos-back.csv:1`): J1 (`screen_connector`, Hirose FH35C-15S-0.3SHW(50), `design/bom.csv:11`), the eight 0201 parts C1, C2, C5, C7, C14, FB1, R71 and R72, with the stencil of `design/ecad/ring/pcb/fab/gerber/ring-B_Paste.gbr`. The 12 test pads TP1, TP3, TP4, TP5, TP6, TP12, TP14, TP15, TP17, TP18, TP19 and TP21 are copper on this face and get no paste (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:96`). A solder bump on a test pad spoils the spring-pin contact of step 5. Today the file `ring-B_Paste.gbr` has a round 0.80 mm opening at each of the 12 test pads (`design/wiring/guide-fix-work/paste_count.log`) and 1:1 openings at J1. The board helper removes the 12 openings and cuts the J1 openings to Hirose's pattern (change C3, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:197`). Until that file is delivered, the stencil maker closes the 12 test pad openings. PASS 2, the PARTS FACE (KiCad F.Cu, 72 parts in `design/ecad/ring/pcb/fab/ring-pos-front.csv:1`), with the stencil of `design/ecad/ring/pcb/fab/gerber/ring-F_Paste.gbr`: U1 (`main_chip`), U2 (`power_chip`, nPM1304, `design/architecture.md:566`), U3 (`storage`), U10, U4 (`screen_supply_chip`), U5 (`mic_1`) and U6 (`mic_2`), Knowles SPK0641HT4H-1 (`design/architecture.md:654`, `design/bom.csv:9`), ANT1 (`antenna`, on the parts face at X -10.80, Y 5.25, on its maker's land pattern, no notch), C89 (not fitted: the pad only) and LT1 (0 ohm) at the antenna (`design/bom.csv:85`, `design/bom.csv:86`), L2, L3, L4, C3, C12, X1 (`crystal_32M`), X2 (`crystal_32k`), L1, R1, R2, C4, C6, C10, C11, L10 (`screen_supply_coil`), C21, C26, C27, C24, C25, C23, C22, C28, C29, C31, C32, C35, C36, R31, R32, R57, R58, R60, C60, U11 (`motor_regulator`, TLV75530PDQNR, at X 0.0, Y 4.2), C83 (`motor_cap_in`, X 0.0, Y 5.45), C84 (`motor_cap_out`, X 1.27, Y 5.0, turned 90) (`design/architecture.md:705`, `design/mech/thin/placement.csv:19` to `design/mech/thin/placement.csv:21`; the board file places them there, `design/ecad/ring/pcb/placement-final.csv:81` to `design/ecad/ring/pcb/placement-final.csv:83`), U9, R61 and R62 (now on U11's enable pin, `design/architecture.md:705`), R66, R63, R64, R65, C61, C72, C80, C81, C82, C71, C73, C74, C75, C78, C79, C76, C77, C85, C86, C87, C88 (`design/bom.csv:67` to `design/bom.csv:69`), L71 (`power_coil_1`), L72 (`power_coil_2`). Two parts are in the parts list and not yet on the board: R73, the 330 kohm VBUS discharge resistor (`design/bom.csv:6`), and C90, a second 10 uF on VDD_3V0 beside C79 (`design/bom.csv:74`). The board helper places them (`design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:77`, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:79`); they are soldered in the pass of the face the position file gives them. The motor switch and the motor diode of earlier versions are not fitted: they left the design (`design/architecture.md:705`). LED1, R41 and Q2 are removed in v7 and are not fitted (`design/architecture.md:834`). C13 is not mounted (`design/bom.csv:18`).
- **The 15 ribbon lands:** J_BAND_A (8 lands) and J_BAND_B (7 lands) on the parts face get paste in PASS 2, one opening per land. Each land leaves the oven with a solder layer of about 0.03 mm. This layer is the solder of the hot-bar joint of step 14 (`design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:23`; `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:55`, `design/ecad/band/band-interface.md:137`). The board finish ENEPIG brings no solder of its own (`design/ecad/ring/pcb/fab/stackup.txt:20`). The 15 openings are not yet in `ring-F_Paste.gbr`: the board helper adds them, sized for the 0.075 mm foil (`design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:228`, `design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:229`). Until then the parts face stencil cannot be cut for this step. These lands add no oven pass.
- **Paste, stencils and squeegee:** one alloy, SAC305 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:55`). Paste Kester NP505-HR, Type 4, 88.5 % metal, no-clean, part 7040211410 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:58`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:59`). Keep the paste in a refrigerator at 0 to 10 C (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:61`). Screen face stencil: stainless steel 0.10 mm, laser cut and electro-polished (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:84`). Parts face stencil: stainless steel 0.075 mm, laser cut and electro-polished (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:85`). Squeegee: a steel or polyurethane blade of 80 to 90 durometer (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:171`).
- **Reflow profile (both passes):** a thermocouple is taped on the board surface beside the most massive part, and the oven follows this profile on it (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:104`). 1. Ramp from room temperature to 150 C at less than 2.5 C per second. 2. Soak from 150 C to 200 C in 90 to 110 s, then go on to 217 C. 3. Stay above 217 C for 60 to 75 s. 4. Stay above 230 C for at most 35 s. 5. Peak 240 C; the thermocouple must read 235 to 245 C. 6. Cool at 6 C per second or slower. Do not move the board while the solder is liquid (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:105` to `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:110`).
- **Never a third reflow:** J1 accepts at most 2 reflow cycles, and the two passes use both. Hirose's drawing says "Number of reflow cycles:2 cycles MAX." (`design/datasheets/w1_hirose_fh35c-15s-0.3shw50_drawing.pdf` page 6, an image page; `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:116`). So the ring board never goes through an oven a third time. The order of the passes: the face reflowed first hangs face down during the second reflow, and the melted solder holds a part there only if the part is small. The screen face has 9 parts, all small (eight 0201 parts and the connector); the parts face has 72, among them U1 and the 1.00 mm parts (`design/architecture.md:426`). How the screen face parts stay on during pass 2: the board lies on a carrier with the screen face down, flat, with a pocket under J1 (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:170`; the housing is 0.9 mm high, `design/wiring/screen-cable-study.md:59`). Nobody touches or moves the board while the solder is liquid. The board is not turned over until it is cold.
- **Locate:** the board itself carries no fiducial (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:185`). By hand: lay the stencil on the board and move it until every opening sits over its land, seen under the 10 times magnifier (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:189`). By a placing machine: the three 1 mm fiducials on the panel rails of the face being printed, 3.85 mm from the panel edges (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:186`; change C4, the panel is not drawn yet). Each part sits on its land pattern of the routed board file (`design/ecad/ring/pcb/board-notes-r3.md:20`). In pass 2 the board lies on its outline in the carrier with the pocket under J1.
- **Before:** step 1 passed. The antenna mount is decided: ANT1 is soldered on its maker's land pattern on the parts face, with no notch (before model v9, ANT1 stood in a corner notch with its pads off the board, `design/architecture.md:513`, open item S15). U3 stays in its sealed bag until it is placed (moisture class 3, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:120`). Before pass 2: the pass 1 check below has passed.
- **Check:** every joint of each pass is judged by these accept rules, under the 10 times magnifier (the lead's wording, after the common workmanship practice for class 2 electronics, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:82`). A joint passes only when all of them hold. 1. The paste has melted and flowed: the solder is smooth and joined up, with no grainy paste left. 2. The solder wets both the land and the metal end or lead of the part: its edge runs out thin onto both, in a hollow curve. 3. No ball of solder sits on top of a land without wetting it, and no bare metal ring shows round the solder. 4. No solder joins two lands that must be apart. 5. No loose solder ball lies next to the joint. 6. The part lies flat on its lands, square to them, and does not stand up on one end. 7. Each end of the part lies on its own land; no end hangs off its land. 8. No crack and no hole shows in the solder. For example, each end of ANT1 has a wetted end with a hollow curve of solder onto its land. Parts whose joints lie under their body cannot be seen: they are X-rayed (below). After PASS 1, also: J1 sits flat on the board with none of its 15 contact tails and its 2 mounting tails lifted; no solder bridge between neighbouring contacts (0.3 mm pitch); each of the eight 0201 parts in place and not tilted; no solder on any of the 12 test pads. After PASS 2, also: X-ray of U1 (61 balls), U2, U4 and the U3 lands, each ball round, of the same size as its neighbours, joined to its land and to no other; each of the 15 ribbon lands carries a smooth solder dome, and no solder joins two of them (`design/mfg/assembly/hotbar-joint.md:171`); then the screen face again: all 9 parts still in place, J1 still flat. A screen face part that fell in pass 2 is put back by hand soldering on that face; the board is never reflowed a third time (`design/mfg/assembly/process.md:116`). J1 itself may be reworked with an iron at 350 C for at most 5 s (Hirose, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:116`). The sheet gives no iron setting for the eight 0201 parts: open, for the lead.
- **After:** reflow is not undone, and the board never sees an oven again. A parts face part can be reworked by hand until step 15, when the board goes into the block. A screen face part can be reworked until step 16, because from step 16 the screen's cable lies over that face.
