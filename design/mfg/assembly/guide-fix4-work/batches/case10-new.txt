Thermistor TH1, then the cell leads W5 and W6 (they come after step 9, because step 9 needs the cell lead W5 not yet soldered: my reading; marks added 2026-11-13, review 2 fault F5; the thermistor check (j) added 2026-11-13 after wiring review 4, fault H3, and TH1 moved before the cell leads so that its check is made on an unpowered board: once W5 and W6 are on, the cell feeds the charger and the board is live). Marks on the bottom face of the main board, same size and style as the five wire labels of step 7 (silkscreen colour assumed white, see Round 3; 1.0 mm high, printed mirrored: read them with the board turned over; script design/wiring/case-marks-2-work/add_marks_2.py, text items in case_main.kicad_pcb): "W5 +" 1.35 mm from the hole W5 (BAT), "W6 -" 1.45 mm from the hole W6 (GND), "TH1" 1.45 mm below the two thermistor holes TH1.1 and TH1.2 (positions in design/wiring/case-marks-2-work/search_B.log). Tools for every joint of this step: Kester 275 SAC305 no-clean cored wire 0.5 mm, part 2470687603 (PM P1.3, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:65`), a 50 W temperature-controlled iron with a chisel tip 1.3 mm wide or narrower, tip at 340 C, at most 7 s per joint (PM P4 table, rows "Case thermistor TH1 leads" and "Case cell leads W5, W6", `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:152`, `design/mfg/assembly/process.md:153`; the cell maker gives no limit, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md:70`), meter with a continuity beep and a resistance range to 100 kohm. (j) Thermistor TH1, the two joints TH1.1 (net TS) and TH1.2 (net GND), board unpowered (no USB plug, cell leads not yet soldered). (j1) Find the measuring point T. The board prints no part names. On the top face, beside the two thermistor holes, two small 0402 resistors sit side by side, RT1 (5.23 kohm) and RT2 (30.1 kohm) (`design/ecad/case/parts.csv` lines 29 and 30; fab/case_main/case_main_position.csv lines 69 to 71: RT1 at X 117.9, Y -82.55, RT2 at X 116.9, Y -82.4, TH1 at X 120.25, Y -81.7, all on the top face). Meter on resistance, black probe on test pad TP16 (GND, as in step 1 (g1)) for every reading of (j): RT2 is the one of the two with a pad that beeps to TP16 (its pad 2 is GND, `design/ecad/case/netlist.csv` line 96); its OTHER pad is the point T (net TS, `design/ecad/case/netlist.csv` line 225). (j2) Before TH1 is fitted: red probe on T. The reading starts low (about 4.5 kohm) and climbs while the capacitor C8 (4.7 uF on REGN, reached through RT1) charges through the meter: wait until it stops changing and write it down as R0. Expected: 29.8 to 30.4 kohm, which is RT2 alone (30.1 kohm, 1 %), if the unpowered charger U2 draws no current at its TS and REGN pins; no file states that, so a steady R0 between 4.5 and 30.4 kohm is accepted and used below; below 4.5 kohm, or above 30.4 kohm, stop: T is the wrong pad or RT1, RT2 or their joints are faulty (arithmetic: design/mfg/assembly/guide-fix4-work/th1_values.py, its log logs/th1_values.log). (j3) Fit TH1: the thermistor leads are bare, with no colour and no polarity: cut to 10 mm, one in each of the two holes at the mark "TH1" (either way round), joints 5 mm or more from the bead, at most 7 seconds at 340 degrees C with a 50 W iron (the tools above). Let the joints cool for a minute and do not hold the bead while measuring: a warm finger lowers its resistance (my reading). (j4) After TH1 is fitted, the same two points, black on TP16 and red on T, wait for a steady reading, R1. The thermistor's own value (`design/datasheets/w16_semitec_P12-13-AT-Thermistor.txt` lines 105 and 182 to 185: 10.0 kohm at 25 C, 12.09 kohm at 20 C, 8.313 kohm at 30 C, about 14.7 kohm at 15 C) lies between 8.2 and 15.1 kohm at a room temperature of 15 to 30 C. Pass: if R0 read 29.8 to 30.4 kohm, R1 must read 6.4 to 10.1 kohm (about 7.5 kohm at 25 C: 30.1 kohm and 10.0 kohm in parallel); for any other R0, work out R0 x R1 / (R0 - R1): it must lie between 8.2 and 15.1 kohm. This reading passes only through both joints (T is on RT2, TP16 on the ground plane; the meter's path runs through TH1.1, the bead and TH1.2), so it proves both. R1 the same as R0: a lead joint is open; reheat both joints and measure again. R1 near 0 ohm: the two leads touch each other or ground; separate them. (j5) By eye at 10 times: the two bare leads touch each other nowhere and touch no other pad, part or the board over their whole length (the leads leave the bead 2.54 mm apart, `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md` line 71, and the holes are 2.0 mm apart, footprint NTC_wired of case_main.kicad_pcb, pads at -1 and +1 mm, so they come a little closer near the board). (k) Cell leads W5 and W6. Lead colours (design/wiring/lead-decisions.md, rows "case cell BT1 leads" and "case thermistor TH1 leads"): the cell's red lead (+) goes through the hole marked "W5 +", cut 13.0 mm; the cell's black lead (-) goes through the hole marked "W6 -", cut 15.2 mm; 2 mm stripped. Before cutting, measure the cell with the red probe on the red lead: the reading must be positive, between 3.0 and 4.35 V; a negative reading means the colours are reversed on that cell, and the cell is set aside. Never swap the two: a reversed cell on the charger is a fault. Solder each lead with the wire, tip and heat named at the start of this step. After the two cell lead joints are made, check them: red probe on pad W5, black probe on pad W6, meter on volts: the reading must again be positive, between 3.0 and 4.35 V, the same as before cutting (PM P8, change C16; values from `design/wiring/lead-decisions.md` line 70, row "case cell BT1 leads": "the reading must be positive, between 3.0 and 4.35 V"). A reading outside this range, or a negative one, means a bad joint or swapped leads: reheat or redo the joint, and never go on with the case until the reading is right (my reading). (l) Last, with the cell lying in its place under the board: each bare thermistor lead to the cell's outside, meter on continuity: no beep, open (`design/wiring/lead-decisions.md` line 71: "checked by eye and with a continuity test to the cell's can: open").
