The product
•

•

•

•
•

A smart ring that records meetings into its own memory. A long press on its one control starts and
stops a recording. A small AMOLED screen sits on top.
Body: titanium grade 5, or stainless steel 316L. Block on top 29.3 x 14.75 x 3.85 mm. Band 8.0 mm wide
and 2.6 mm thick. Seven sizes, US 7 to US 13.
Inside: Nordic nRF54LM20A Bluetooth LE chip, Nordic nPM1304 power chip, 2 MEMS microphones, 4 GB
storage, a vibration motor, a five-way joystick, a custom curved lithium polymer cell (4.35 V type, 18
to 32 mAh by ring size).
The inside of the band is closed by a resin liner cast in place. Four charging pads sit in the liner.
Charging case: USB-C, 700 mAh cell, four spring pins. It charges the ring and moves the recordings
over USB.
