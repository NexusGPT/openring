Every time stamp below is written by the `date` command (Mac clock, CET). The server clock is UTC, one hour behind. No git is run (the brief forbids it), so a time stamp, not a commit, shows that this section came before the first solver run. Server is written <build-server>.

## 0. PRE-REGISTRATION (frozen at 2026-11-13 00:25:34 CET; nothing in this section changes after the first solver run)

### 0.1 Hypotheses (verbatim from the brief)
H1 (the tool can be trusted): "An open-source field solver, set up by you, reproduces the antenna maker's own published figures for the antenna on the maker's evaluation board."
H2 (the design): "The same antenna on the ring's board, inside the ring's body, worn on a finger, reaches a return loss of 6 dB or better across 2.40 to 2.48 GHz with a three-part match, and radiates at least 10 percent of the power it accepts."
Order: H1 first. If H1 fails, H2 is run only as "tool not validated".

### 0.2 Pass line H1 (written from the maker's datasheet before any run)
Maker figures, evaluation board 2450AT18D0100001CE1, file design/datasheets/antenna_johanson_2450AT18D0100001E_8p.pdf: page 1 "Return Loss (dB) 6 Min.", "Peak Gain (dBi) 1.5 Typ.", "Average Gain (dBi) -1.0 Typ.", "Average Radiated Efficiency (%) 72"; page 4 markers "m1 freq=2.400GHz dB(S(1,1))=-13.935", "m2 freq=2.500GHz dB(S(1,1))=-14.774", "m3 freq=2.450GHz dB(S(1,1))=-24.894". Board 40 x 20 mm, clearance "No GND on all layers" 4 mm wide and 6 mm deep from the board edge (page 3).
The tuning step (0.4) sets the one free number so that the S11 minimum sits at the maker's 2.450 GHz. After it, H1 is confirmed only if ALL of P1 to P4 hold in the same run (the brief says "peak gain or efficiency": where it reads two ways I take the stricter, both must hold):
- P1: simulated return loss at 2.44 GHz is at least 10 dB (S11 at most -10 dB). Maker typical is 24.9 dB at 2.45 GHz. Tolerance: 14.9 dB below the typical value, because the depth of a notch is set by sub-0.1 mm details of the part's inside, which the datasheet does not give; 10 dB is the maker's own minimum in its 2023 sheet (design/datasheets/antenna_johanson_2450AT18D0100001E.pdf page 1, "Return Loss 10 dB min") and the usual "well matched" level.
- P2: simulated return loss at 2.40 GHz and at 2.50 GHz is at least 6 dB each. Maker typical 13.9 and 14.8 dB; the limit is the maker's own 8-page "6 dB Min." (page 1). Tolerance 7.9 and 8.8 dB. This tests the bandwidth, which the tuning step does not set.
- P3: radiation efficiency (power radiated in the far field divided by the power the port accepts) at 2.44 GHz between 52 percent and 92 percent. Maker 72 percent. Tolerance plus or minus 20 points, because the maker's figure is a chamber measurement of an unspecified method, the model has an assumed loss tangent and PEC copper, and a gap of 10 to 20 points between such a model and a chamber is usual.
- P4: peak gain (directivity times radiation efficiency, mismatch not counted) at 2.44 GHz between -1.0 and +4.0 dBi. Maker 1.5 dBi typical. Tolerance plus or minus 2.5 dB.
Reported but not part of the pass line: average gain (maker -1.0 dBi).

### 0.3 Falsifier H1
Any of P1 to P4 outside its limit in the verdict run, or no run among at most three tuning runs whose S11 minimum lies in 2.440 to 2.460 GHz (then "H1 not confirmed: the one free number could not set the resonance"). A run that crashes or ends without reaching the end criterion is recorded as itself; its numbers are used only if the energy has fallen to -30 dB or lower (the run then says so), otherwise H1 is "cannot tell" and that run counts in the eight.

### 0.4 Method fixed for H1
Solver: openEMS (FDTD) with its Python interface, in Docker on <build-server>, image ring-antenna-openems:1 built from server/Dockerfile (Ubuntu 24.04, openEMS-Project cloned and built with update_openEMS.sh --python --disable-GUI, as docs/install_clone-build-install.txt and docs/install_auto_install.txt say). Run command: `nice -n 19 openEMS` style call inside the container, limit 55 minutes by `timeout`, excitation Gaussian centre 2.5 GHz cut-off 0.6 GHz (covers 1.9 to 3.1 GHz), end criterion 1e-5 (energy -50 dB), step cap per run set in run script, absorbing boundary PML 8 cells.
Board (not all given by the maker, so ASSUMED and not tuned): 40 x 20 mm, board edge with the antenna at y = 0, antenna centred on the long edge (maker's "center-edge mount", page 1 and page 3 drawing). FR-4 0.8 mm, eps_r 4.4, loss tangent 0.02 (ASSUMED: the datasheet gives no thickness or material). Copper as PEC sheets: ground on the bottom face, none on the top face except the lands and the feed line. Clearance 4 x 6 mm at the antenna edge: no copper on either face (page 3). Maker's lands 1 / 2 / 1 mm across and 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.6 mm deep (page 3). Feed: 50 ohm microstrip, width 1.5 mm (Hammerstad-Jensen gives 50.65 ohm for h 0.8, eps_r 4.4, logs/ms_z0.log), from the board's left edge to x = -4, then a neck 0.4 mm wide to the feed land. The maker's grounded coplanar line and via fence are NOT modelled (simplification S-H1-1): the grounded lands are joined to the bottom ground by PEC vias placed outside the clearance. Port: openEMS microstrip port 50 ohm at the left end of the line.
Antenna: 3.2 x 1.6 x 1.2 mm block (page 2), ceramic eps_r = the one tuned unknown (start 14, allowed 4 to 40), loss tangent 0.005 (ASSUMED, a typical LTCC value, not tuned), four bottom terminals a = 0.3 along the length and b = 0.5 across (page 2) as PEC pads; one feed terminal (the one on the side away from the board edge, at the left end, as the maker's drawing page 3 routes the line to it), three grounded terminals (page 2 "Terminal Configuration"). The inside of the part is not published: ASSUMED radiator = a meandered PEC strip 0.2 mm wide, six legs along the width 1.2 mm long, pitch 0.56 mm, in a plane 0.15 mm under the block's top, joined to the feed terminal by a vertical PEC post, open at the far end (about 11 mm of conductor). This geometry is fixed and not tuned; only eps_r is.
Tuning rule (the only change allowed between H1 runs): run 1 at eps_r 14. Next eps_r = previous eps_r times (f_res / 2.450)^2, where f_res is the frequency of the S11 minimum in 2.0 to 3.0 GHz (a first-order rule: resonance goes as 1 over the square root of the permittivity). Stop at the first run with f_res in 2.440 to 2.460 GHz: that run is the verdict run. At most three H1 runs. The final eps_r is then HELD FIXED for every H2 run.
Output: S11 from 2.0 to 3.0 GHz (step 5 MHz or finer), efficiency and gain at 2.44 GHz and 2.45 GHz from the solver's near-to-far-field box.
Mesh rule: cell at most 0.1 mm in the antenna block and its lands (x, y), 0.1 mm in z through the block and 0.2 mm through the board, graded by factor 1.3 (smooth mesh) to at most 2.5 mm in air (maximum 1/40 of the wavelength at 3 GHz); air margin 18 mm on every side of the board; the mesh is reported (cells, steps, end value, run time) per run.

### 0.5 Pass line H2 (from the brief; copied word for word, then the rules that turn data into a verdict)
Brief: "reaches a return loss of 6 dB or better across 2.40 to 2.48 GHz with a three-part match, and radiates at least 10 percent of the power it accepts." Plus the brief's case list: "Run with the body as designed. Then run two more cases the design files discuss: the body corner near the antenna replaced by resin, and the antenna at the best other place you can justify in one sentence." For each case: S11 before matching, the three match values that give the best S11, S11 after matching, radiated share of the accepted power.
Rules written now (they turn the simulated numbers into a verdict):
- Frequencies: 2.400 to 2.480 GHz, S11 evaluated every 1 MHz (81 points), every point must have S11 at most -6 dB after the match ("across" read as every point).
- Three-part match: ideal lumped parts, topology shunt, series, shunt (the "pi (or shunt-series-shunt)" network the maker asks for, 8p PDF page 3 footnote 4), each part an ideal inductor or capacitor with positive value, source side 50 ohm (assumed: the radio pin's own network is outside this simulation). The script searches all 8 inductor/capacitor patterns and values from 0.2 to 20 nH or 0.1 to 10 pF with a minimax objective (smallest worst S11 over the 81 points). "Best" = that minimum. Values are reported as solved, and also snapped to the nearest 0201 values in the 0.1 pF and 0.1 nH steps of the parts list; the verdict uses the unsnapped values and reports the snapped S11 beside it.
- Radiated share = radiation efficiency = radiated power from the near-to-far-field box divided by the power the antenna port accepts (before the match; the ideal match parts are lossless, so it does not change). Needed: at least 10 percent at 2.40, 2.44 and 2.48 GHz each.
- Case verdict = pass only if both the return-loss rule and the radiated-share rule hold. Each of the three cases gets its own verdict. If H1 is not confirmed, each is labelled "tool not validated" next to its numbers.
- Everything is "simulated, not proven on hardware".
