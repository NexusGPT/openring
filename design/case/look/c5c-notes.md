# C5c: the soft cube, finished and detailed (2026-11-11)

Built in place from C5b (`design/case/case.py`, `case_check.py`, `out/`, `look/`). C6 (`design/case/c6/`) untouched; ported from it: the revolved dish, the stepped pocket, the roll test, the one-arc crown, tests 11 and 12. Old pictures kept as `look/case-closed-c5b.png`, `case-open-c5b.png`, `case-open-empty-c5b.png`, `case-side-c5b.png` (the C5b/C6 compare picture is `look/case-compare-c4-c5b.png` of the earlier round).

## Size and what it costs
Outside 45.4 x 45.9 x 41.4 mm (`out/case_size.txt`), C5b was 45.5 x 45.9 x 41.4: no growth (limit 45.5 x 46.0 x 41.5). Parts: 35 nodes against 29 (+6: `liner`, `usb_bezel`, `light_window`, `foot`, `seam_trim`, `lid_seam_trim`). Wall: unchanged 1.2 to 1.5 mm; the liner (0.9 mm) sits in the dish and the shell floor under it is 0.9 mm (floor slab 1.8 mm = 0.9 shell + 0.9 liner).

## Each detail
1. Lid. Top edge an ellipse 10.0 x 1.83 mm that ends at 60 degrees (r = 17.5 mm), then ONE circular crown arc (radius 165 mm, sag 0.94 mm) tangent to it, to the apex at 41.4 mm: zero slope only at the apex, no flat ring, no second rise (C5b's crown was a smooth step). Thumb dimple: a spherical cap 8.0 mm across, 0.4 mm deep (sphere radius 20.2 mm) cut along the surface normal at (18.5, 11.4) mm, the crown opposite the pivot; it is part of `lid`. Tests 11 and 12 (new, failing): largest angle between neighbour facets of the lid (apex fan within 1 mm of the axis and the dimple's 6 mm circle left out) at most 2 degrees; z(r) of the crown along y = 0 from the apex to r = 21 mm has no inflection. C5b's lid measured 2.71 degrees and 7 inflections on the same measures (`design/case/c6/look/check_final3.log`, INFO lines).
2. Seam. 0.3 mm gap kept (z 37.66 / 37.96). Two polished bands, each 0.4 mm high x 0.4 mm deep along the whole outline with a 0.2 mm chamfer on the seam edge, own parts: `seam_trim` (base) and `lid_seam_trim` (lid, turns with the lid). 24.7 mm3 each. The bands replace the outer 0.4 mm of the wall at the seam, so no size or wall cost.
3. Inside. `liner`: the C6 revolved dish about the post (floor flat, 1.8 mm roll, wall flaring from 17.1 to 18.3 mm radius), 0.9 mm thick, 2041 mm3, soft dark; it is clear of the ring at every size US 7 to 13 (the dish radius is the ring and joystick-nub envelope plus the 0.3 mm clearance, with the joystick angle per size 84 / 81 / 78 degrees; check test 2a). The pocket follows the block (half width 7.54 mm = 7.38 + 0.15 in the narrow part, wide only in the 12.4 mm flare slab); roll test is a failing test at every size US 7 to 13 (pad row shift at most 0.50 mm; the check's lines "roll play ... OK"). V flanks: they stay and are not rounded: they are the two flat faces the band rests on (tangent at +-20 degrees); a fillet on the cut shell could add material on a concave edge and take clearance from the band, and I could not prove it in the time. They are still the roughest looking feature (two bars through the dish).
4. USB-C. `usb_bezel`: a 0.4 mm wide dark frame round a 10.14 x 4.51 mm rounded slot (corner radius 0.9 mm), its face 0.2 mm inside the skin (the frame is cut from the skin pushed in 0.2 mm), 15.5 mm3. The socket front stays 0.3 mm behind the skin at its outermost corner. `light_window`: I chose the flush 1.0 mm dot (a light pipe from the skin to the LED, 1.0 mm dia): the check's seam test and the C5b contract already fix a 1.0 mm dot; a 3 x 0.8 mm bar would need a wider hole next to the bezel (0.5 mm wall now between dot and bezel), so the dot is kept. The LED moved to y = 6.0 mm (U1 shifted 0.6 mm in y) to make room.
5. Base. `foot`: a soft ring following the plan between 5.0 and 7.5 mm inset (not 3 mm: the crowned base is only 0.44 to 0.6 mm above the ground there; at 3 mm inset it would be 1.0 mm proud), 0.57 mm proud at most, 134 mm3. The marking field: a rounded 14 x 8 mm recess in the bottom crown, floor at z = 0.35 mm (0.35 mm deep at the centre, about 0.2 mm at its ends), no text; it also removes the single point where the crowned base touched the ground.
6. `pin_insert`: not built. The pins stand on a three-island PCB2 whose outer islands are recessed by 0.4 mm; an insert round the barrels needs that board redone, and the present lug is a plain white block in the pictures but reads tidy next to the dark liner. Pins and pad contact are unchanged.
7. Kept: size, 700 mAh cell, board, hidden pivot, magnets, no screw visible, joystick angle per ring size.

## Second round (lead's rulings) and final check
Final model rebuilt once (`look/c5c_build8.log`), checked, then meshes and all six pictures made from it. Size 45.4 x 45.9 x 41.4 mm. Check log `look/c5c_check_final.log`, last line: `case_check: PASS, 3071 pairs checked, 0 failures, ring sizes US [7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13]`.
- Test 11 split. 11a dome (z above 39.58 mm, dimple circle and apex fan left out): 1.47 degrees, limit 2. 11b edge band (z 38.73 to 39.58 mm): ellipse radius of curvature VT^2/ET = 0.336 mm (limit 0.30), facet angle 5.54 degrees (limit 10). Can-fail proof (`look/c5c_test11_breaks.log`): a lid with a 0.4 mm score across the dome gives 11a 92.99 degrees, FAIL. The sharp-edge copy could not be built (the loft stalled 20 minutes, I stopped it), so 11b is shown failing only by its radius formula (0.15 mm edge gives 0.002 mm, FAIL); the facet-angle half of 11b has not been shown to fail.
- Lid swing. Lid band now built without the boolean cut (`band_prism`, the seam trims wrap it). The solid-to-solid distance still returned 0.0 on some pairs, so the check measures face by face of the moving lid, plus the common volume of the lid with every part and the rings at every step. Calibration (`look/c5c_calib_swing.log`): lid in place 0.3 mm to shell, liner and seam trim, common volume 0; pushed 0.5 mm into the shell: distance 0.0 and common volume 161 mm3 (FAIL by both). The check also fails any 0.000 reading. Result: ring 0.600 mm, other parts 0.300 mm, common volume 0.000000 mm3. (I did not rerun the committed C5b lid for the calibration; the in-place lid of C5c reads the same 0.3 mm.)
- V rests are now owned by `liner` (the flank prisms are added to the liner and cut from the shell; the floor and flank measures in the check read shell plus liner). Roll lines identical to c5c_check3.log (0.50 to 0.75 degrees, shift at most 0.119 mm). In the picture they are dark bars, no longer white.
- Dimple: 1.5 mm tangent rim blend built; lid meshed at 0.006 mm; no star in the pictures.
- Renderer: ring body satin titanium, glass black; post satin like the shell; pin board and wings matt black (BOM lines for PCB2 and the post updated); underside re-aimed three-quarter from below.
- Not done: `pin_insert`; rounding of the V rests (they stay: the band's seat, a fillet could take clearance); the sharp-edge can-fail copy for 11b angle. Remaining weak: the post lug is plain, the underside view shows the cube from low but the foot and field are small.

Save point: all work done, nothing running. case-architecture.md: pin table row now Harwin (not confirmed), ring charge text now 15 mA / 17.6 to 31.9 mAh; the cell-sizing numbers after it still use 15 to 20 mAh (flagged, not recomputed). PAD_X_OFF unchanged.

RESULT: pass, C5c 45.4 x 45.9 x 41.4 mm, case_check: PASS, 3071 pairs checked, 0 failures, ring sizes US [7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13]; pin_insert and V-rest rounding not done.
