1. What the product is

  • A smart ring that records meetings. Recording starts only on a long press (1.0 s) of its one control, a five-way joystick with a round cap on the side of the band. The ring has no recording light: the lit screen shows that it records.
  • A flat block on top holds a small screen under a formed cover glass (black print, no opening), the main circuit board, two microphones and the storage.
  • The band holds a curved battery, a vibration motor, the joystick's switches and four charging pads on its inside face, under a resin liner cast in place.
  • The ring charges and moves its recordings over USB through the four pads, in a charging case.
  • Seven ring sizes, US 7 to US 13. The block and everything in it are the same at every size; the band, the battery length and four positions change by size.

5. What is not in the package

  • Datasheets of bought parts. They belong to their makers; the parts list names each part and its maker.
  • Circuit board fabrication files (they exist in the project but are not in this package yet), the circuit board work files, and software code.
  • Full tolerance drawings: the sheets inside are overview sheets (section 3).
  • The recording light, its light guide and light plug, and the rectangular joystick nub: removed in version 7; their old files are not included.
  • The phone application and its link to the ring: not part of this request.

6. Open points a supplier must know, and can help close

  • Circuit board: the board is routed and passes its rules check (0 violations, 0 unconnected items); it has not been built or tested. The three removed parts (R41, LED1, Q2) are no longer in the board files.
  • Case spring pin: the Harwin P70-6000045R is proposed, not confirmed (its stroke is not in the files read; the contact window 2.15 to 2.65 mm is an assumption).
  • Pin holder: 0.79 mm walls between the four bores, in black LCP (Vectra E130i); no moulder has confirmed them. The two heat stakes are untested, and the touch electrode reads through a 1.97 mm LCP wall (bench item).
  • Cell: real capacity, charge and discharge limits, protection circuit, bend radius per size.
  • Finish: which gloss black coating holds on a ring that is worn every day, its thickness and gloss; whether the frame bond (3M Scotch-Weld DP420 Black) holds on the coating.
  • Body: the thinnest walls and the two 0.20 mm rings under the microphone seats; the window between the motor pocket and the block on sizes US 7, 8 and 9; the antenna sits under metal, and a resin corner is one proposed answer.
  • Recording sign: the screen shows that the ring records; the power of that always-lit sign has no number yet, so the recording time is not final.
  • Thin-border screen: version 1 uses the 0.95 inch part and hides its dark border under black glass. For a later version we ask screen makers for a flexible version of this panel with the drive electronics folded behind it, or a custom panel: lit area as close as possible to 27.9 x 13.4 mm, border about 1 mm, 0.8 mm thick or less, 4-wire SPI. Please state engineering fee, minimum order and lead time.
  • Screen cable: the screen maker's cable is 12.4 mm wide where it folds; the body has 5.4 mm for it. A narrowed cable is asked for (item S27).
  • Antenna mount: the antenna is soldered on the board at its hand end corner, inside a copper-free area, and the body has a resin corner over it (the owner kept the resin corner). Simulated, not proven: the range is measured on the first built ring.
  • Liner: a 0.3 mm resin skin cast in place over the battery and the cables, with the cables embedded; casting in two shots is proposed.
  • Seals: glass to body, cable slots, motor window, joystick; materials and method.
  • Microphone parts: a membrane of at most 0.10 mm and a foam gasket in three thicknesses.

7. How to read the 3D files

  • Units are millimetres. X runs along the finger, Y across it, Z up through the screen. The joystick is on the minus Y side.
  • Angles round the band are counted from the top of the ring; the joystick sits 78 degrees round on US 9 to US 13, 81 degrees on US 8 and 84 degrees on US 7.
  • The whole ring exists per size in 02-3d/maker-files/assembly/. The values that change by size are also listed in the model notes, section "Model v7".
  • The joystick cap and the joystick frame are one file each, placed at the US 9 position (minus 78 degrees); the same shape serves all sizes.
