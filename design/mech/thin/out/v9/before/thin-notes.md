## Node contract
One top-level node per placement row, named as the row's `part`; children `<part>__<class>`, one child per class that the part has (all solids of a class in one mesh). Classes: metal, gold, pcb, chip, ceramic, polymer, glass, screen, foam, flex, cell, resin, mark. Top-level nodes carry extras (reference, layer, metal, angle_deg, centre, explode_dir); children carry extras.class; scene extras repeat layers.json and the class list. The GLB's own materials (one per class) are a preview only. Meshes are indexed, with smooth normals inside each B-rep face and hard edges between faces, built at linear deflection 0.005 and angular deflection 0.1 rad; the solder balls use 0.45 rad (0.2 to 0.25 mm balls, 61 + 35 + 16 of them) to stay under the triangle limit.
