The test is section 13 of check_thin.py (function `check_band_circuits`, called from `main`). It measures each ribbon on the built solids (bisection on `is_inside`, radial scans, bounding boxes) and compares with values written in the test (neck 1.8 at X -3.4 to -1.6 in the slot, the trunk, the pad strip X -3.4 to 0.25, the foot X -3.5 to -1.0 with inboard end at Y 3.4, foot top Z 1.40 and thickness 0.11, fold radii 0.70 and 0.81 at 30 and 60 degrees, fold width 2.15 at 45 degrees, foot stiffener, head stiffener thickness 0.10, joint space 0.03 under the board land faces, 8 board lands over the foot) and with the circuits' own file design/ecad/band/band_geom.py (TAIL_W, TAIL_X, R_FOLD_IN, T_FLEX, JOINT, LF), read only with bytecode writing switched off. The neck is also centred in the slot measured on the body. A change of the circuits' width, fold or joint in band_geom.py, or of any one place in the model, gives a line starting `BAND circuit` in the findings.
